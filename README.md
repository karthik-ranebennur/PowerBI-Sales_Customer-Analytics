Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

Project Overview

An end-to-end Sales & Customer Analytics project developed using Power BI, SQL, Power Query, and DAX. This project analyzes sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and regional targets to support data-driven business decisions.

Business Objectives

* Analyze total sales, costs, profit, and profit margin.
* Track sales target achievement and variance.
* Evaluate customer purchasing behavior and repeat purchases.
* Identify high-performing products and categories.
* Compare sales performance across regions and employees.
* Analyze order status and quantity sold.
